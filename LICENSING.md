# Licensing in this repository

The starter tooling, validators, schema, contributor documentation, and
starter infrastructure in this repository are available under the
[MIT License](LICENSE).

That repository licence does **not** replace a pack's own licence. Packs under
`packs/**`, their content, and their assets may use different licences. The
example and reference packs may also carry their own declared licences.

Third-party assets retain their original licences and attribution conditions.
For a pack, `pack.json` is the authoritative location for pack-content and
asset licensing/provenance metadata. The MIT licence never grants rights to
third-party text, data, images, or other content included in a pack.

Technical validation checks metadata completeness only. It does not verify
legal rights, ownership, or licence suitability.

For original community pack content, OH Play prefers CC BY-NC-SA 4.0
(`CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0`). The creator remains the rightsholder and can separately
grant commercial permission for their own work. This preference does not
override third-party asset licences or grant rights in game mechanics.
